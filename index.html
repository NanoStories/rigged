Rigged

Every pair of letters is joined by an arc, red above or blue below. Whenever three letters are joined by arcs of one color, the House marks the triangle in gold. Click an arc to flip it.

You will lose.

Each game here hands you a challenge that looks perfectly doable. Try it. When you lose, ask the House how it beat you. Its answer is a proof, and it runs on your own losing position.

  • Six Dots

    Color every line between six dots red or blue without making a triangle that’s all one color.

    Play

  • Every Prime

    Write down every prime number. All of them. The House will check your list.

    Play

  • Nobody Wins

    Fill a Hex board with red and blue so that neither color can cross from one side to the other.

    Play

Rigged is a small, free arcade of mathematical games, designed and built by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. More tables are on the way. The source code is on GitHub.

Set in Besley by Owen Earl and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next by the Braille Institute, both under the SIL Open Font License.
